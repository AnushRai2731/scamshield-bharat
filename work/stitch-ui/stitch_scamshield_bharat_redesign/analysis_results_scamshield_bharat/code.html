Executive Copilot Summary

This message is a widespread social engineering scam targeting utility consumers across India. The sender is attempting to panic you into installing a malicious remote-access app under the guise of an electricity bill update. State electricity boards (e.g., BESCOM, MSEDCL, UPPCL, TANGEDCO) never issue disconnection notices via informal WhatsApp numbers.

Signal 1: Artificial Urgency & Fear Trigger

High Alarm

"Power supply will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid previous month bill." Scammers invoke tight deadlines at odd hours to induce panic, bypassing rational judgment before you can verify with family or customer support.

phonelink_erase

Signal 2: Unverified Personal Communication Channel

Spoofed Channel

Sent from a standard 10-digit prepaid Indian SIM card (+91 98321-XXXXX) or standard WhatsApp account, instead of an official DLT-registered government Discom SMS header (such as VK-BESCOM, BP-TSSPDCL, or BZ-MSEDCL).

dangerous

Signal 3: Sideloaded APK Payload / Unofficial Link

Zero Legitimacy

Direct installation files (.apk) bypass Google Play Protect policies and request high-risk permissions: SMS read access (intercepting banking OTPs), screen casting, and call monitoring.

Direct Action Protocols: What to Avoid vs. What to Do

cancel

STRICTLY DO NOT

Prevent immediate compromise
  • do_not_disturb_on Do NOT click any web link or download any file ending in .apk sent by the caller or sender.
  • phone_disabled Do NOT call back the phone number provided in the SMS or WhatsApp message.
  • currency_rupee_circle Do NOT make even a ₹1 or ₹10 "verification" payment. This is a credential harvesting trick to log your secure UPI PIN.
  • key_off Do NOT share any SMS OTP or approve unexpected UPI push approval requests on PhonePe, GPay, or Paytm.
check_circle

RECOMMENDED ACTION

Safe civic resolution path
  • verified_user Open your official electricity board app or visit the official state portal directly (e.g. bescom.karnataka.gov.in).
  • receipt_long Verify your bill status using your unique 10-digit Consumer Account Number (CA/K No) printed on your physical bill.
  • block Block & report the contact on WhatsApp or SMS app as "Spam / Fraud" to protect other citizens on telecom networks.
  • support_agent Call the official customer care helpline printed directly on your last physical paper bill if in doubt.
