Deconstructed Scam Blueprints

Rampant in MH, KA, DL Vector: SMS / APK Sideload

Electricity Suspension / State Discom Scam

Victim receives a message claiming bill non-payment with impending disconnection at 9:30 PM. They are directed to call a fraudulent number or install “QuickSupport” APK, giving attackers full screen access to harvest banking OTPs.

shield The ScamShield Antidote

Discoms provide official disconnection notices at least 15 days in advance via registered mail. Never install any file ending in .apk.

High Surge on NH Tolls Vector: Spoofed Search Ad / Phishing

FASTag Expiry & Blacklist Fraud

SMS claims FASTag account is blocked due to missing KYC. Clicking the link leads to an identical bank payment portal where submitting the OTP results in unauthorized auto-debit registration.

shield The ScamShield Antidote

Only recharge FASTag via official bank apps or NPCI authorized Bharat BillPay (BBPS). FASTag KYC never requires remote app installation.

Targeting Homemakers & Students Vector: Telegram / YouTube Review

YouTube Likes & Hotel Rating “Tasks”

Starts with ₹150 payout for rating 3 Google Maps places. Once trust is gained, the victim is added to a VIP Telegram group and lured into depositing ₹10,000+ for “Crypto arbitrage commissions” which cannot be withdrawn.

shield The ScamShield Antidote

No legitimate employer asks candidates to deposit upfront money to release earnings. Cease contact as soon as “pre-paid task balances” are mentioned.

Targeting Senior Citizens Vector: Impersonation / Call

Aadhaar Sim Block & “Digital Arrest”

Scammers posing as Telecom Regulatory Authority (TRAI) or Mumbai Cyber Police claim the victim's Aadhaar has 23 illicit SIM cards linked to money laundering. They coerce the victim to stay on a continuous video call under “Digital Arrest”.

shield The ScamShield Antidote

The Supreme Court and MHA have clarified: “Digital Arrest” does not exist under Indian Law. Police never conduct judicial custody over Skype or WhatsApp video.
